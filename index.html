A LETTER

For Trinetra

Dear Trinetra,

Some things are easy to say. Some things are easier to feel. And then there are the things I feel for you that I don't think I'll ever be able to explain completely with words. But I still want to try.

When I first saw you, something felt different. I can't even explain what it was. It wasn't just a normal feeling, and it wasn't something I had felt before. Somehow, you stayed in my mind. I kept thinking about you, and little by little, that feeling became something much more beautiful.

Then we met. We talked. We got closer. And eventually, I gathered all the courage I had and told you how I felt.

And that moment will always have a special place in my heart.

Because you were the first person I ever told “I love you” to, and you were the first person who told me “I love you” back.

You were my first love.

And somehow, even after you became my girl, I didn't stop falling for you. I just kept falling more. Every single day.

You became the first thought in my mind when I wake up. The first thing I want to see is whether you've messaged me. And somehow, even the smallest things remind me of you.

Your name alone is enough to put a smile on my face.

You came into my life and brought something I didn't even realize I had been missing. Light.

Before you, there were days that felt dark and empty. But then you came along, and somehow, everything started looking a little more beautiful. You filled those dark places with colors I didn't know existed.

You gave me a kind of love I never thought I would experience. And that means more to me than I could ever properly tell you.

You somehow took a guy who was serious, cold, a little too much of a gangster and boxer, and turned him into the biggest Pookie ever.

Now I hear your name and suddenly I'm blushing like a red tomato, hiding my face, smiling for absolutely no reason, and probably dancing around like an idiot.

And honestly? I wouldn't change that for anything.

Because that's what loving you feels like.

You make me happy in a way that feels completely different from anything I've ever known.

And whenever I think about you, there's always one thought that comes back to me:

“Please let me keep her in my life.”

Because if having you in my life can make me this happy, then I want to spend my life making sure you feel that same happiness too.

I want to see you smile. I want to see you achieve everything you've ever dreamed of.

I want to see you buy the things you've always wanted without having to hesitate about whether you can afford them.

I want to build a beautiful life with you — one where we can look around one day and realize that everything we once dreamed about became real.

And I promise you, I'll give my best to make that future beautiful.

Not because I have to. Because you deserve it.

I want to be there for you. I want to take care of you. I want to celebrate your happiness, stand beside you through the difficult days, and be the person you can always count on.

And I don't want this to just be a beautiful chapter. I want you to be part of every chapter that comes after this one.

Because when I say that I love you, I don't mean it as just three words. I mean every little thing behind them.

I mean the memories. The smiles. The waiting for your messages. The way your name makes me smile. The way you changed me without even trying. The way you brought colors into my life. The way you made me believe in love again.

And the way you became my first love — the love I'll always hold closest to my heart.

So, Trinetra...

This Sai Aashish wants to be yours. Forever.

And I don't want to just ask you to be mine for today. I want you beside me for all the tomorrows we're going to create together.

I want us to grow together, dream together, laugh together, build our little world together, and one day look back at this letter and smile because we actually made everything we dreamed about come true.

And yes...

I want to marry you.

Will you marry me?

Not as some random promise thrown into the air, but as the future I want to build with you.

I see you beside me. I see us creating a beautiful life together. I see the two of us looking back at where everything started — at this little world, at this letter, at these feelings — and realizing that our story became everything we hoped it would be.

So I'll keep choosing you. I'll keep loving you. I'll keep trying my absolute best to make our future brighter and more colorful.

And whenever the world gets difficult, remember one thing:

I've got you.

No matter how hard the day gets, you won't have to face it feeling alone.

Because you are not just someone I love. You're the person who brought my smile back. You're my first love. You're my Pookie. You're the person who turned my world into something brighter.

And you mean more to me than these words could ever hold.

So, one last time, from the most honest part of my heart:

I love you.

A lot. A lot more than I know how to explain.

And this Sai Aashish...

wants to be yours forever.

Always keep smiling, okay? Because your smile is one of my favorite things in this entire little world.

Forever yours,
— Aashish ❤️
